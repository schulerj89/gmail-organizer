# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in Gmail Organizer respectful, welcoming, and useful for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or perspective.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, patient, and constructive.
- Discuss ideas and code without attacking the person behind them.
- Respect privacy, especially when discussing email, credentials, or security reports.
- Give and receive feedback with the goal of improving the project.
- Assume good intent while remaining clear about technical and safety concerns.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Sexualized language or unwanted attention.
- Deliberate disruption, spam, or repeated bad-faith participation.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and comments that violate this code. Serious or repeated violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through their GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and confidentially as practical.
